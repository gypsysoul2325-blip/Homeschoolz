Unit 1: Course Launch + Act I, Scene 1 — Part 1

Oct 1–14, 2026

📖 Exact Reading

Act I Scene 1 from beginning through end of conversation about Troy’s job/garbage-truck complaint; stop before conversation shifts to Alberta and Cory football opportunity.

🧠 Vocabulary — choose 5 of 8

For each chosen term: define it in your own words, explain its connection to this unit/Fences, and write one original sentence.

stage directionsdialogueexpositionsettingcharacterizationconflictmotivationinference

4 Focus Days

Day 1 — Read & Notice

Complete the exact reading. Mark 3 important lines, details, or stage directions and explain what each reveals.

Day 2 — Vocabulary & Close Reading

Complete 5 vocabulary terms. Study 2 marked passages and connect each to a character, conflict, theme, or author choice.

Day 3 — Analysis & Evidence

Skill: Explain the most important idea with a clear claim, at least 2 specific pieces of evidence, and explanation. Do not just summarize. Answer the unit questions using evidence and explanation.

  1. What is the most important idea?
  2. Which character, line, event, or stage direction best supports it?
  3. What does the text show beyond simple plot summary?
  4. How does this connect to an earlier part of Fences?
  5. What interpretation or question should you carry forward?
Day 4 — Writing & Finish

Explain the most important idea with a clear claim, at least 2 specific pieces of evidence, and explanation. Then check claim, evidence, explanation, organization, and grammar. After that, complete the Unit Check.

✅ Unit Check — Show What You Know

Open-note and not timed. Use the book, notes, vocabulary, and reading log. Answer in your own words and correct anything you are unsure about.

  1. Explain stage directions.
  2. Describe Troy using at least 2 pieces of evidence.
  3. Explain the conflict.
  4. Explain the setting.
  5. Find one stage direction and explain what it tells you.
  6. Find one dialogue line revealing Troy’s attitude toward work/fairness.
  7. Find one relationship detail.
  8. Create a character snapshot using evidence.
  9. Make a claim about Troy with 2 evidence pieces and explanations.
  10. Why begin with an ordinary conversation?
  11. Choose 3 vocabulary terms and explain how each appears.
