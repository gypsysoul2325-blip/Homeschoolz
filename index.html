📘 English 11 — Literature, Writing & Language

Fences by August Wilson • 2026–2027

One book: Fences by August Wilson. No Gatsby and no second novel.

10 units • Every unit has 4 focused English workdays.

📖 Ongoing Reading Habit

On other days, read about 15 minutes and keep a reading log: what you read, one detail, and one question or thought.

📝 Vocabulary Rule

Each unit gives 8 purposeful vocabulary/literary terms. Choose 5. For each: define it in your own words, explain its connection to this unit/Fences, and write one original sentence.

⭐ Focus Day Routine

  1. Day 1 — Read & Notice: mark 3 important lines/details/stage directions and explain what each reveals.
  2. Day 2 — Vocabulary & Close Reading: complete 5 terms and study 2 marked passages.
  3. Day 3 — Analysis & Evidence: answer the unit analysis questions; choose strong evidence and explain it. Do not summarize.
  4. Day 4 — Writing & Finish: complete the unit assignment; check claim/evidence/explanation/organization/grammar; then complete the Unit Check.

🏁 Final Course Reflection

  1. What major theme do you understand now? Give evidence.
  2. Which character changed your understanding the most?
  3. What is the most important scene?
  4. How did a symbol or motif develop in meaning?
  5. What does Wilson do well as a playwright?
  6. How have you grown in using evidence and explanation?
  7. What writing skill improved?
  8. What skill should you continue practicing?
  9. What is your final confidence level?
